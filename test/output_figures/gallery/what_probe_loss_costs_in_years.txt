What probe loss costs, in years

14 clocks · platforms: EPICv1, EPICv2 · measured, not assumed

Full 450K matrices masked down to each platform's probe set and rescored,
absent probes filled from each clock's reference values as score() fills them;
the bar is the median shift and the whisker a bootstrap interval. A bar at
zero is a clock that lost nothing. A long bar is a clock whose lost probes
carry weight no fill value stands in for, which is why a correlation check
passes while the intercept moves. Reported, never subtracted.
