Survival by age acceleration

phenoage · 500 subjects · 277 events · log-rank p < 0.001

Strata are the tails of the acceleration distribution, not a median split: the
published figure compares the fastest-ageing tenth with the slowest, because
the middle of the distribution is where a clock has least to say. Curves that
cross mean the hazard is not proportional, and the hazard ratio beside them is
then an average over a period during which the ordering changed.
