Clock atlas across pooled studies

40 clocks · 13 studies · 394 samples · 12 of 193 comparisons significant at BH q < 0.05

Read panel D down the page. A row of hollow circles on zero is an algorithm
that detected nothing in any cohort; filled dots pushed right are the ones
that did, and a row with several says the effect held across studies rather
than in one. Panels B and C are diagnostics for reading D, not scores: a clock
that merely returned chronological age would be perfect in B, empty in D, and
useless. Panel F separates the two ways of finding nothing: a clock that saw
its features and detected no difference, from one that never had the features
to look with. Panel G is for the clocks whose scale admits no acceleration at
all (a pace, a telomere length, a division count) and asks the question their
scale does allow: were cases and controls separated. Cliff's delta, so 0 is no
separation and 1 is complete, and unlike D it is not adjusted for age, which
matters when a cohort's cases are older than its controls.
