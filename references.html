References

Every source the documentation cites, numbered once

Citations across this site are numbers in square brackets and every one of them points here. The numbering is global rather than per page, so a source keeps its number wherever it is cited, and the same list is bound into the PDF as its own chapter.

Where a source is available at a stable identifier the entry links to it. Some of these are preprints and are cited as preprints on purpose: the field moves faster than journal review, and a claim resting on one is worth knowing about.
